        How the math works

        Avios, Asia Miles and Atmos Rewards all price award seats using fixed charts based on how far you fly. We measure the great-circle distance from your airport, look up each chart, then find the cheapest way to pay using miles you already hold first and then the bank points worth the least to you.

        °/1k pts is how many degrees warmer than home you get for every 1,000 points spent.

        The fine print

        Prices are estimates for a direct oneworld flight and don't check seat availability, taxes or fees. Charts and transfer ratios change; ours were last checked recently. Point values are our own estimates.
